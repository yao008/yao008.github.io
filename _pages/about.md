I am a lecturer at the Nanyang Centre for Public Administration, Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore. I received my PhD in public policy and global affairs from NTU, supervised by Prof. [Jue Wang](https://dr.ntu.edu.sg/cris/rp/rp00240). My research focuses on artificial intelligence, technology governance, and public administration. In particular, I study how generative AI affects human decision-making, professional work, scientific collaboration, and public-sector governance.
